# METCUT v1 — Data Sources

---

## Alaska boreal–tundra plant isotope dataset (ABT)

**Source:** O'Connor, K. F., Berke, M. A., & Ziolkowski, L. A. (2020).  
**Article:** *Hydrogen isotope fractionation in modern plants along a boreal-tundra transect in Alaska.* Organic Geochemistry, 147, 104064.  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1016/j.orggeochem.2020.104064  
**Role:** Site 4 plant/xylem/plant-wax empirical comparison and broader Alaska transect context.  

## ERA5-Land

**Source:** Muñoz Sabater, J. (2019), ERA5-Land hourly data.  
**Provider:** Copernicus Climate Change Service Climate Data Store.  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.e2161bac  
**Role:** Hourly meteorological and hydrologic forcing; air temperature, precipitation, pressure, radiation, wind, soil-water and lower-boundary fields.  

## IsoGSM4-HR

**Source:** Bong & Yoshimura (2025), IsoGSM-HR atmospheric water isotope dataset.  
**Development folder:** `IsoGSM4_HR/`  
**Role:** Site 4 atmospheric vapor and precipitation δD forcing.  

## UCBG leaf architecture and functional traits

**Source:** Matos, I. S. et al. (2024). *Leaf architecture and functional traits for 122 species at the University of California Botanical Garden at Berkeley.* Ecology 105, e4436.  
**Dataset DOI:** https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.1g1jwsv36  
**Role:** Empirical leaf geometry and venation domain used to test the spatial leaf-water transport formulation and the reduced coordinate `qleaf = aeff/L`.  

## ROOTd rooting-depth compilation

**Source:** Schenk & Jackson (2002) and associated rooting-profile compilations.  
**Role:** D50/D95-type rooting depth and rooting-profile conditioning.  

## TRY Plant Trait Database

**Source:** Kattge et al. (2020), TRY Plant Trait Database.  
**Role:** Leaf dimensions, orientation, vegetation height, trait context, and related morphological conditioning during development and the Site 4 application.  

## Tundra Trait Team (TTT)

**Source:** Bjorkman et al. (2018).  
**Role:** Tundra structural/trait context.  

## MODIS MCD15A3H LAI/FPAR

**Source:** Myneni, Knyazikhin & Park (2021), MODIS/Terra+Aqua LAI/FPAR 4-Day L4 Global 500 m SIN Grid V061.  
**Role:** LAI/canopy structural conditioning.  

## ABoVE plant functional type top cover

**Source:** Macander & Nelson (2022), *ABoVE: Modeled Top Cover by Plant Functional Type over Alaska and Yukon, 1985–2020.*  
**Role:** PFT/canopy structural context.  

## Interior Alaska NDVI / species cover / LAI

**Source:** Fiore et al. (2021).  
**Role:** Alaska vegetation structure, cover, NDVI, and LAI context.  

## SATFiD tundra field database

**Source:** Chen et al. (2023), *Field Data on Soils, Vegetation, and Fire History for Alaska Tundra Sites, 1972–2020.*  
**Role:** Alaska tundra vegetation/site context.  

## Seward Peninsula structural traits

**Source:** Serbin et al. (2023), leaf structural/chemical traits, vegetation temperature, and height, Seward Peninsula, Alaska.  
**Role:** Structural and environmental context used during Site 4 conditioning.  

## CLIMOOR

**Source:** Beier et al. (2000), CLIMOOR climate-manipulation dataset/report.  
**Role:** Gas-exchange and stomatal-conductance plausibility envelopes, including Empetrum observations used in physiological screening.  

## Vaccinium hydraulic / physiological sources

- Anadon-Rosell et al. (2017)
- Ganthaler & Mayr (2015a)
- Ganthaler & Mayr (2015b)


## LOPEX93

**Sources:** Hosgood et al. (1993, 1995).  
**Role:** Leaf reflectance/transmittance spectra used for shortwave absorptance conditioning.  

## ASTM G173

**Source:** ASTM G173 reference solar spectral irradiance.  
**Role:** Solar spectral weighting of leaf optical properties.  

## Boreal/peatland plant optical spectra

**Source:** Salko et al. (development files use `Salko2024`; manuscript citation uses the final published source/year).  
**Role:** Boreal/peatland leaf reflectance/transmittance constraints and taxon-specific optical conditioning.

## Other Rosa/Coldfoot context sources

Development also used Alaska DOT&PF/FHWA riparian vegetation surveys, Alaska Department of Fish and Game native-plant guidance, BLM Utility Corridor vegetation descriptions, a Wishbone Hill vegetation chapter, and other near-site/locality reports. These sources provided occurrence, botanical stature, or habitat context but did not supply accepted site-specific canopy-height parameters in the inspected registries.
